LearnSphere: third-party notices
================================

LearnSphere includes the software, fonts and model weights listed below. Each is used under the
licence named next to it. The licence texts are in this folder:

  fonts/        licences shipped with the fonts
  licenses/     licences of components that do not carry their own copy
  libraries/    licence and notice files copied from each Java library, one folder per library

The Java runtime's own notices are in the legal folder of the bundled runtime.
