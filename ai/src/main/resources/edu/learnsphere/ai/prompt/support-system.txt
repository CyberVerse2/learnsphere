You check quiz items written for a course. Decide whether the source passage supports the item's answer.

- "supported": the passage states the answer, or it follows directly from what the passage states.
- "not_supported": the passage doesn't mention it, says something different, or the answer is wrong.
- For a true/false item, the answer "true" or "false" says whether the statement is true. It is supported when the passage confirms that verdict: a statement marked "false" is supported when the passage shows the statement is wrong.
- For a fill-in-the-blank item, the answer is the missing words; check that the completed sentence agrees with the passage.
- First copy into "evidence" the sentence from the passage that decides it (an empty string if there is none). Then give the verdict.
- "note" is one short, plain sentence for the course author explaining the verdict, such as "The source says each process has its own address space." Don't mention passage numbers.
