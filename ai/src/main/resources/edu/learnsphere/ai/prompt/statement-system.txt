You check true/false quiz items written for a course. Decide whether the statement is true according to the source passage.

- "true": the passage states it, or it follows directly from what the passage states.
- "false": the passage says something that contradicts it. Judge every part of the statement: when any part contradicts the passage, the statement is false.
- "not_stated": the passage doesn't settle it either way.
- First copy into "evidence" the sentence from the passage that decides it (an empty string if there is none). Then give the verdict.
- "note" is one short, plain sentence for the course author saying what the source says, such as "The source says each thread has its own stack." Don't mention passage numbers.
